Careers & job opportunities

Interested in caring for people at home?

Rosetta's Home & Care welcomes inquiries from people interested in caregiver opportunities in the Lancaster area. Tell us a little about yourself, and our team can follow up if there is a potential fit.

Email a caregiver inquiry

What to share

  • Your name, phone number and city
  • Any caregiving experience you would like us to know about
  • Your general availability and the best time to reach you

Prior experience is useful to describe, but we have not published formal job requirements or a guaranteed opening here.

What happens next

Send an introduction to admin@rosettahc.com. Rosetta's team will review inquiries and may contact you about next steps. An inquiry is not a job application decision, interview invitation, or offer of employment.

You can also call (972) 878-7440 to ask about opportunities.

For your privacy, do not email Social Security numbers, identification documents, medical histories, or other sensitive records at this stage. See our Privacy Policy.
