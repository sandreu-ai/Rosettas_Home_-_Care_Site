Home care services designed with you in mind.

Rosetta's Home & Care offers personalized, compassionate support for everyday needs at home.

Personal Care Services

Respectful daily support with routines that help clients feel comfortable and cared for.

  • Feeding assistance
  • Grooming support
  • Dressing assistance

Light Housekeeping

Practical help keeping the home environment clean, organized, and manageable.

  • Dusting, sweeping, mopping
  • Dishes and counters
  • Light laundry and trash removal

Meal Prep

Meal support based on preferences, dietary needs, portions, and cleanup after meals.

  • Meal planning support
  • Prepared meals
  • Kitchen cleanup

Medication Reminders

Clear reminders and routine support to help clients stay on schedule.

  • Pill organizer reminders
  • Alarm or note reminders
  • Phone check-ins where appropriate

Running Errands

Help with the errands that make daily life easier for clients and families.

  • Grocery shopping
  • Prescription pickup
  • Mail, packages, and household essentials

Transportation & Appointments

Support organizing appointments and getting to important errands or visits.

  • Transportation support
  • Appointment reminders
  • Coordination with family members
