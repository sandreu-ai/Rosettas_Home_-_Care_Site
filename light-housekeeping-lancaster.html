Light housekeeping at home

Rosetta's Home & Care includes light housekeeping in its non-medical support: help with routine cleaning, dishes, laundry, and tidying to keep everyday tasks manageable.

Request a free consultationCall (972) 878-7440

What tasks are included?

The current website lists dusting, sweeping, mopping, dishes and counters, light laundry, and trash removal. These are examples of routine household help rather than a promise of every cleaning task. Ask about the specific rooms, tasks, and frequency that matter to your family.

How can this fit into a care plan?

Light housekeeping may be discussed alongside meal preparation, errands, or personal care. During a consultation, explain which daily routines are hardest to keep up with and whether help is needed for one person or a household. The team can clarify what support and timing are available.

What should you ask before starting?

Confirm the scope of each visit, the schedule, service location, and payment arrangements directly. Do not assume that specialized cleaning, heavy lifting, medical care, or insurance reimbursement is included simply because light housekeeping is listed on a home-care website.

Scope note: This page lists light, routine household support. It does not advertise deep cleaning, hazardous cleanup, or medical treatment.
