Where does Rosetta's provide home care?

Families in these five communities can ask about the same non-medical care categories: personal care, light housekeeping, meal preparation, medication reminders, errands, and transportation or appointment support. The exact scope is discussed during a consultation.

Lancaster, TX

Rosetta's office is at 1425 N Dallas Ave STE 304-A, Lancaster, TX 75134. Lancaster families can contact the team about help with daily routines at home, including personal care, meals, or household tasks.

DeSoto, TX

DeSoto is part of Rosetta's home-care service area. If a family member needs support with dressing, grooming, or meals at home, describe the type of help and the DeSoto service address when requesting a consultation.

Duncanville, TX

Rosetta's also serves Duncanville. Families seeking routine housekeeping, errands, or support coordinating appointments can ask which tasks and visit times are available for their household.

Ovilla, TX

Ovilla is within the stated service area. For a care inquiry, tell Rosetta's team the home location, the type of everyday assistance needed, and when it would be useful to start; availability is confirmed in follow-up.

Cedar Hill, TX

Cedar Hill households can ask about non-medical support such as meal preparation, personal care, and transportation or appointment coordination. Confirm the travel and scheduling details directly before relying on a particular arrangement.

How to get started

Call the Lancaster office or use the free consultation request on the homepage. Mention your city and the type of assistance you are exploring. Do not include diagnoses or medical records in the website form. A request does not itself book care, establish coverage, or guarantee a schedule.
