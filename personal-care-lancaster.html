Personal care at home

Rosetta's Home & Care offers non-medical help with everyday personal routines, including dressing, grooming, and feeding assistance. The goal is practical, respectful support in familiar surroundings.

What does personal care include?

Personal care focuses on day-to-day tasks that may be harder to manage alone. Rosetta's existing service list includes feeding assistance, grooming support, and help with dressing. A conversation about your family's needs is the right place to clarify which tasks are available, when help is needed, and what the next step would be.

When should a family ask about help?

If getting ready for the day or keeping up with a personal routine is becoming difficult, ask about support before assuming a particular schedule or care plan. Explain the type of assistance needed, your city, and a good time to talk. You do not need to send diagnoses or medical records through the website form.

What happens after an inquiry?

Call or request a free consultation, describe the daily-living support you are seeking, and discuss timing and availability directly with Rosetta's team. Submitting a form starts an inquiry; it does not itself confirm services, a schedule, insurance coverage, or a booking.

Care boundary: This page describes non-medical personal care. It does not promise nursing, medication administration, diagnosis, or emergency treatment. For a medical emergency, call 911.
